Retail and shop fitouts, Sydney

We build shops. Shopfronts, display and counter joinery, lighting, flooring and signage installation, inside Westfield, Stockland and Scentre centres and on the high street, for national brands and first-time retailers alike.

What we build

  • Shopfronts, glazing and roller shutters
  • Display, counter and point-of-sale joinery
  • Lighting layouts, track and feature lighting
  • Flooring, wall finishes and fitting rooms
  • Signage installation and shop drawings
  • Centre out-of-hours program, landlord approvals and defects

Program

A refresh of an existing shop runs three to five weeks; a new-tenancy fitout with services eight to twelve. Centre fitout guides, hoarding and night work are built into the program.

Approvals

Inside a shopping centre the landlord's tenancy coordinator approves the design and the centre's own rules apply. A new shopfront on the street or a change of use can need complying development through a private certifier; we handle it.

The City Fitouts approach

01

Price

We walk the site with you and your drawings, measure what is there, and come back with a fixed price and a program. Not an estimate that grows - a number you can sign a lease against.

02

Build

Our own site manager runs the job every day. Licensed trades, a certifier engaged from the start, centre and landlord conditions handled, and weekly progress reports with photos.

Questions people ask

Have you worked in the major centres?

Yes - Macpac at Warringah Mall and Kareela Community Pharmacy are both centre fitouts, built on the centre's out-of-hours program and handed over for the opening date.

Do you build from the brand's standard drawings?

Yes. National brands send a standard fitout package; we price it against the actual tenancy and build it to the brand's specification.

Can you do a roll-out?

Yes. Several tenancies on one program with the same team and the same pricing basis.
