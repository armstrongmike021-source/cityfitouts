Pharmacy fitouts

Pharmacy fitouts, Sydney

A pharmacy is a shop at the front and a clinical space at the back. City Fitouts builds both: retail shelving, gondolas and lighting that sell the front of store, and a dispensary with the sinks, refrigeration, secure storage and consult room the Pharmacy Council of NSW requires. Kareela Community Pharmacy is one of ours.

What we build

  • Dispensary joinery, sinks and vaccine refrigeration
  • Secure storage for scheduled medicines and a lockable dispensary
  • Consultation rooms for vaccinations and services
  • Retail gondolas, wall bays and feature lighting
  • Shopfront, signage and security
  • Compounding rooms where needed

Program

A refresh of a trading pharmacy runs four to six weeks, often staged so the scripts keep coming; a new pharmacy in a shell eight to twelve.

Approvals

A pharmacy replacing a shop is retail to retail, so the internal work is usually exempt and a new shopfront complying development. Pharmacy Council of NSW premises registration covers the dispensary, storage, refrigeration and a private area for counselling; the PBS approval is the owner's matter.

Questions people ask

Can we stay open during the refit?

Usually. We stage the retail floor and do the dispensary after hours, as at Kareela, so the pharmacy keeps trading.

Do you build to the banner group's manual?

Yes. Priceline, Chemist Warehouse, TerryWhite and the independents' banners each have a fixtures and graphics standard; we build to it.

What about the dispensing robot?

Your supplier sets the footprint and the services; we build the floor, power and data to it so it drops in at the end of the job.

How much does a pharmacy fitout cost?

Roughly $1,800-4,200 per m2 in 2026, between a shop and a medical fitout. The cost checker gives the range for your floor area.
