Questions people ask

Do you do the design as well?

No. City Fitouts is a builder. We build from your architect's, designer's or shopfitter's drawings, and we are happy to price early sketches so the design can be adjusted to the budget before it is finished. If you do not have a designer yet we can point you to several we have built for.

How long does a fitout take?

A basic office or shop refresh runs four to six weeks on site; a full hospitality or medical fitout twelve to twenty. The cost checker gives a program for your space type and finish.

Do I need council approval?

Often not. Internal, non-structural work in a commercial building is usually exempt under the NSW Codes SEPP. A change of use, a new shopfront or a food premises can go through a private certifier as complying development. Heritage buildings and some changes of use need a DA. We engage the certifier and manage the approval as part of the job.

Will the price change?

Only if the scope does. The lump sum covers everything on the drawings and in the scope list; anything the base building hides (asbestos, undersized services) is flagged the day we find it, priced, and only done when you say so.
