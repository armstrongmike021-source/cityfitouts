Medical and dental fitouts

Medical and dental fitouts, Sydney

Medical rooms carry requirements an office never sees: infection-control surfaces, medical gases, dental plumbing and suction, lead lining for imaging, accessibility to every room. City Fitouts builds GP, dental, specialist and allied-health practices with those details costed in.

What we build

  • Consult, treatment and procedure rooms
  • Dental surgeries with suction, compressed air and plumbing to the chair
  • Reception, waiting and accessible amenities
  • Infection-control finishes, hand-wash stations and sterilisation rooms
  • Specialised electrical, data and mechanical
  • Imaging rooms with shielding where required

Program

A medical suite in an existing health tenancy runs eight to ten weeks; a new practice with dental chairs or imaging fourteen to twenty.

Approvals

A health use in a tenancy that was not one before is a change of use and usually needs complying development or a DA. Accessibility (AS 1428) applies to the whole fitout. We engage the certifier and the access consultant early.

Questions people ask

Do you coordinate with the dental equipment supplier?

Yes. The chair, suction and compressor suppliers give us their service points and we build the floor and wall penetrations to them before the slab is closed.

Can a shop become a medical centre?

Often, but it is a change of use with parking, accessibility and sometimes acoustic requirements. We check the tenancy before you sign the lease.

What about the base-building services?

Medical rooms draw more power and air than offices. We check the switchboard and mechanical capacity at the walk-through, so the price includes any upgrade.
