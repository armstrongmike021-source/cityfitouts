Childcare centre fitouts

Childcare centre fitouts, Sydney

A childcare centre is built around the regulations: unencumbered space per child, a bathroom for every playroom group, a cot room, a kitchen, a laundry and the outdoor area. City Fitouts builds new centres and converts existing buildings for operators and developers.

What we build

  • Playrooms, cot rooms and sleep rooms
  • Children's bathrooms and nappy-change areas for each group
  • Commercial kitchen and laundry
  • Staff rooms, office and parent reception
  • Outdoor play areas, soft-fall and shade
  • Fencing, access control and fire services

Program

Converting an existing building runs ten to fourteen weeks; a full new centre fitout eighteen to twenty-four. The program is built around the operator's licensing inspection date.

Approvals

A new centre always needs a DA, and the Education and Care Services National Regulations set the spaces. The provider approval and service approval from the NSW regulator follow the build. We work to the operator's consultant and the approved plans.

Questions people ask

Can you convert a house or a shop into a centre?

Sometimes. It needs a DA and the right zoning, parking and outdoor area. We walk the building with you before you commit.

Do you build the outdoor area?

Yes - soft-fall, shade structures, fencing, landscaping and the covered verandah are part of the job.

How much does a childcare fitout cost?

Roughly $2,000-4,200 per m2 of indoor area in 2026, before the outdoor area. The cost checker gives the range for your floor area.
