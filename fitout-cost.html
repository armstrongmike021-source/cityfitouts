What drives the number

  • Services - mechanical, electrical, hydraulics - are the biggest swing.
  • A commercial kitchen and exhaust roughly doubles a hospitality budget.
  • Joinery is where premium money goes.
  • The base-building condition (make-good state) matters.
  • Approvals add time more than money.
