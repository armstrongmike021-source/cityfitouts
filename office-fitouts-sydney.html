Office fitouts, Sydney

From a 100 m2 suite to a whole floor: City Fitouts builds office fitouts across Sydney for tenants, landlords and the architects and designers who draw them. Fixed price, fixed program, one site manager.

What we build

  • Partitions and glazed offices, meeting rooms and boardrooms
  • Ceilings, acoustic treatment, lighting and power
  • Reception and kitchen joinery, breakout and collaboration areas
  • Mechanical, fire and data coordinated with the base building
  • Flooring, painting and feature finishes
  • Make-good at the end of the lease

Program

A basic refresh runs four to six weeks on site; a full new-partition fitout with services nine to fourteen. Out-of-hours work in occupied buildings is priced in, not added later.

Approvals

Most internal office work is exempt development under the NSW Codes SEPP. A new use, a mezzanine or work in a heritage building goes to a private certifier as complying development or to council. We engage the certifier and run the approval as part of the job.

Questions people ask

Do you work in occupied buildings?

Yes. Most office fitouts happen while the rest of the building trades. We work to the building manager's rules - after-hours noisy work, protected lifts and lobbies, dust control - and price it from the start.

Can you price before the design is finished?

Yes, and it is the best time. We price your designer's sketch set so the design can be trimmed to the budget before the drawings are complete.

Do you do make-good?

Yes. We price the make-good against the lease and the landlord's schedule, so the tenant hands back exactly what is required and no more.
